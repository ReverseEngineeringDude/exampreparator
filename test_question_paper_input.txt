First Series Examination
Course: Internet of Things (IoT)
Maximum Marks: 46 (Suggested)
Time: 2 Hours
PART A: Very Short Answer Questions (1 Mark Each)
Answer all questions. Each question carries 1 mark.
1. Define the term Internet of Things (IoT).
2. What specifically constitutes a "Thing" in the context of an IoT ecosystem?
3. State one primary difference between the MQTT and CoAP protocols.
4. What does the acronym BLE stand for in IoT networking?
5. State one key feature that differentiates an IoT system from a standard network.
PART B: Short Answer Questions (3 Marks Each)
Answer all questions. Each question carries 3 marks.
6. List and briefly explain three essential features of an IoT system.
7. Describe three major real-world applications of IoT.
8. Compare and contrast the CoAP and MQTT protocols (provide at least three points of
comparison).
PART C: Essay Type Questions (7 Marks Each)
Answer all questions. (In a real exam, you might offer internal choices like "Answer any 3 out of
4"). Each question carries 7 marks.
9. Draw and explain the various layers of the IoT Stack in detail, highlighting the function of
each layer.
10. ] Discuss the different design Levels of IoT. Support your answer with appropriate block
diagrams or examples for each level.
11. Explain the MQTT protocol in detail. Describe its architecture, publish-subscribe
mechanism, and role in IoT.
12. Discuss the Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) protocol. Explain its working principles and why it
